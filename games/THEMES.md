# The Whole Counsel: Theme Registry

Kojo's locked direction (Sept 16, 2026): **every book has its own unique theme and color.**
No two books share a primary palette or motif. Each theme carries: CSS palette
variables, a background texture/motif, the book's cover art, and optional
book-specific features. All artwork original, nothing copied.

Builders: check this file before theming a new book. Claim a palette + motif that
no book above has taken. Add your book's entry when done.

## Palette ideas (suggestions only, not locked)

- Leviticus: bronze and altar-fire ember on dark stone (sacrifice, holiness)
- Psalms: deep harp-wood brown with dawn gold (worship, song)
- Proverbs: scroll parchment and olive (wisdom)
- Isaiah: royal purple and coal-fire red (prophecy, the coal)
- John: warm light breaking through deep blue (the Word, light)
- Revelation: throne white-gold and crystalline blue (glory)
- Ruth: harvest wheat gold and field green (redemption in the fields)
- Jonah: storm grey-green and deep-sea blue (the deep)
