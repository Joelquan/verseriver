Salvation Stories

Real, documented stories of how people came to believe in the Lord Jesus. Every story records the actual conversion, with its sources attached, from peoples and languages across the earth.

How this collection is built: each story must document the actual conversion, how, when, and under what circumstances the person came to believe. Stories of persecution or later Christian work alone do not qualify. Testimonies that have been credibly challenged are excluded, and records that are uncertain or sensitive are flagged in place. Where the record is thin, the gap is left empty.

Salvation Stories is a KnowHoly collection, hosted on Verse River. Sources are listed inside each story.
